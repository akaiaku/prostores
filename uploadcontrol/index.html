Personal video publishing tool

Plan once, post your videos everywhere.

Upload Control keeps each of your videos, with its title, caption, hashtags, thumbnail and schedule, in one place, and posts it to your own TikTok and YouTube accounts when you click post.

What it does

One video, every platform

Write the title, description and tags once. Override them per platform when TikTok or YouTube needs something different.

Post to TikTok

Connect your TikTok account with Login Kit and publish with the Content Posting API, using the privacy, comment, Duet and Stitch settings you choose.

Upload to YouTube

Upload with a custom thumbnail, add to a playlist and schedule the public release time.

Track status

See every video as Draft, Ready, Scheduled, Published or Failed on each platform, with a link to the live post.

Multiple accounts

Connect more than one channel or account and pick which one each video goes to.

Private by design

Runs on your own computer. Videos, captions and tokens are stored locally, with no analytics or tracking.

How posting to TikTok works

  1. Connect your accountSign in with TikTok and approve access. The app reads your basic profile (display name, avatar) to show which account you are posting to.
  2. Prepare the videoAdd the video file, caption and hashtags. The app checks caption length against TikTok's limits.
  3. Choose posting settingsPick who can view the video and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed. Only the options your account allows are shown.
  4. Confirm and postNothing is posted until you review the preview and click post. You can then follow the upload status and open the published video.

You can disconnect your TikTok account in the app at any time, which deletes its stored tokens, or revoke access from your TikTok settings.
